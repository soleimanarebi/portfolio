About

I research at the intersection of AI safety, control theory and nonlinear dynamics. My research asks how to guide complex systems toward reliable outcomes by choosing which parts to influence, how many interventions are needed, and how to design those interventions.

I apply these ideas to teams of language-model agents: treating communication as a network and verification as a control input. The goal is practical oversight—finding which agents a verifier should correct, and how few corrections are needed to help the group reach a reliable answer.

My work spans nonlinear network control, multi-agent AI safety, and experimental systems. I've built hardware testbeds for formation control and synchronization, and run experiments involving tens of thousands of Claude calls.

Research

  1. Controllability of LLM Agent Networks →

    Which agents to verify, and how many. In a pilot with 30 Claude agents and 50,400 model calls, placing verifiers by betweenness centrality cut final error by 13 to 17 points over random placement (p = 0.001). A fitted linear consensus model predicted held-out graphs with r = 0.92.

    • SDRE
    • Centrality

  2. Controlling LLM Agent Collectives →

    Multi-agent LLM debate modelled as a networked dynamical system: network topology, verifier placement and coordination dynamics, with pilots on GSM8K and AIME. Includes bounds for runaway agents and capture by compromised verifiers.

    • Multi-agent LLMs
    • Consensus dynamics
    • Bootstrap · Wilcoxon

  3. Pinning Control of Nonlinear Complex Networks ↗

    Synchronization of nonlinear complex dynamical networks by pinning a few nodes with suboptimal SDRE controllers, and selecting driver nodes by network centrality. Published in Nonlinear Dynamics.

    • Nonlinear control
    • Lyapunov
    • SDRE
    • MATLAB

  4. Networked Control Testbeds

    Hardware for testing nonlinear and networked control: a ground–air formation control setup, a quadrotor hardware-in-the-loop rig and a servomotor synchronization platform.

    • Formation control
    • Synchronization
    • HIL
