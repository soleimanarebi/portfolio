Steering networks of agents, machines and energy systems

I'm a control theorist working on oversight of multi-agent AI systems. My PhD built pinning control and controllability methods for nonlinear complex networks. I now apply them to teams of language-model agents, treating verifiers as control inputs and the communication graph as the network.

01 · about

About

I bring 18 years of modelling, simulation and data-driven control across research and industry: robotics and mechatronics testbeds at K. N. Toosi University of Technology, CFD for solar power plants, AI-driven HVAC control, and today thermal and structural engineering of building façades in London.

My current research asks a practical question about AI oversight: in a network of language-model agents, which agents should a verifier correct, and how few are enough for the whole group to reach the right answer?

02 · research

Research areas

ai.ml

AI and machine learning

Controllability of LLM agent networks. A pilot with 30 Claude agents and 50,400 model calls. Placing verifiers by betweenness centrality beat random placement by 13 to 17 points of final error (p = 0.001), and a fitted linear consensus model predicted held-out graphs with correlation 0.92.

A second study models multi-agent LLM debate on GSM8K and AIME as a networked dynamical system. Earlier: an AI/ML digital twin for data-driven HVAC control at Leaftech, genetic algorithms and fuzzy modelling.

network.control

Network control

Pinning control, synchronization and controllability of nonlinear complex networks: choosing driver nodes by network centrality and designing suboptimal SDRE controllers. Published in Nonlinear Dynamics (2016).

Built testbeds for networked and nonlinear control: a ground–air formation setup, a quadrotor hardware-in-the-loop rig, a servomotor synchronization platform and a 7-DOF manipulator.
